Server logs

When you open a page, the web server records technical data (IP address, time, requested file, browser) to deliver the pages and keep them secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The logs are kept only as long as needed for this purpose.

No tracking, no cookies

There are no analytics, ads or cookies. Fonts and sounds are served from this server. Your progress and settings are stored only in your browser (local storage); you can delete them at any time by clearing the site data.

Camera in ConductJemp

Only if you start the camera: the video is analysed on your device and is neither transmitted nor stored. To do this, the hand-recognition software is loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net and storage.googleapis.com (Google); these providers receive your IP address.
